Privacy Policy

Effective Date: October 25, 2026 · Rapidsellers Dot Prints

Zero-Cookie & Privacy-First Policy

Rapidsellers Dot Prints does NOT use any cookies, tracking pixels, or third-party behavioral analytics cookies on this website. We do not track your browsing habits, build user profiles, or sell your information. You can browse and use our platform with complete confidence that your privacy is respected.

1. Who We Are

Rapidsellers Dot Prints ("we", "us", or "our") provides high-speed shipping label generation, dot-matrix and thermal label optimization, and print formatting tools for marketplace sellers on Flipkart and partner platforms.

2. Order Processing Automation (Coming Soon)

Please note that our comprehensive Order Processing Automation Suite is currently in active development and coming soon. The current live service focuses solely on Rapidsellers Dot Prints for ultra-fast shipping label formatting, batch processing, and marketplace dispatch compliance.

3. Information We Collect

Because we operate on a privacy-minimalist architecture:

  • No Web Cookies: We do not place tracking cookies, advertising identifiers, or cross-site surveillance beacons on your browser.
  • Marketplace Label Data: To generate and format dot prints, we process required marketplace shipping labels and order barcodes. This operational data is handled strictly to generate print-ready formats and is not retained longer than required for dispatch generation.
  • Account & Communication Details: If you register or reach out for assistance, we store basic contact information (such as your seller email and account identifier) exclusively to deliver services and customer support.

4. How We Use Information

Information processed by Rapidsellers Dot Prints is used strictly to:

  • Convert Flipkart and marketplace order manifests into high-speed Dot Print formats.
  • Prevent label misalignment, duplicate barcodes, and printing delays.
  • Provide technical customer assistance and service updates.

5. Data Sharing & Third Parties

We do not sell, rent, trade, or share your data with advertisers or data brokers. Data is transmitted securely only to verified marketplace APIs (e.g. Flipkart) as authorized by you to fulfill order label processing.

6. Data Security

We implement TLS/HTTPS encryption across all data transmissions. Access to production systems is strictly restricted to authorized operational infrastructure.
